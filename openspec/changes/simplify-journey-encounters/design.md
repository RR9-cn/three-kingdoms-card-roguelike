# Design

The base journey uses a repeated cadence: three score checks followed by a boss, then three more score checks followed by the final boss. Ordinary battles increase numerical pressure and let builds express their normal behavior. Bosses provide the memorable rule exceptions.

This change removes `high-armor`, `variety`, and `flank` from stages 3, 6, and 7 without deleting their core implementations. It changes no targets or rewards, allowing simulation differences to be attributed to removing encounter penalties.
