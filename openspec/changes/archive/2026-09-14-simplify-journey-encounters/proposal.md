# Simplify Journey Encounters

## Why

Five of the eight campaign battles currently alter scoring or selection rules. This makes ordinary encounters compete with the two bosses for attention and gives the player too little room to see their own build operate normally.

## What Changes

- Make stages 3, 6, and 7 pure score checks while preserving their current targets.
- Keep rule-changing encounters concentrated at Zhang Bao and Zhang Jiao.
- Retain the existing inactive modifier implementations for possible later high-difficulty or boss use.

## Impact

Stage data, campaign copy, rules documentation, and encounter tests change. General abilities, scoring formulas, economy, targets, bosses, saves, and controls remain unchanged.
